Internal Personnel Guide

Staff Frequently Asked Questions

Internal guidelines regarding operational scaling, administrative compliance, equipment red-tagging, and field protocols.

Q1: What is the strict protocol for escalating internal grievances or administrative questions?

Personnel must strictly scale issues vertically through the Chain of Command (Step 1: On-duty Supervisor → Step 2: Manager → Step 3: Executive Command). Bypassing tiers ("chain-skipping") introduces administrative friction and can accumulate toward formal disciplinary strike logs.

Q2: When is an operator authorized to execute an emergency tactical override?

An operator may break vertical CoC communication scaling only during unmitigated life-safety threats: active secondary collision risks inside a closed corridor, imminent structural hazards threatening ground emergency teams, or sudden criminal scene escalations when no supervisor is present.

Q3: What actions trigger immediate roster removal or disciplinary review?

Failing to clear all four practical cadet evaluations within the 14-day hard window, conducting unauthorized traffic stops or detainments (violating the jurisdiction matrix), carrying unauthorized weapons on duty, or operating state assets off-duty for personal recreation results in instant termination.

Q4: How are equipment red-tags and mechanical vehicle defects handled?

Supervisory staff hold exclusive authority to red-tag faulty fleet equipment or heavy machinery. Operators discovering mechanical failures must immediately log the defect in the internal maintenance database, secure the asset, and swap to a fully verified secondary unit.

Q5: What are the mandatory logging and activity quotas for active operators?

All active roster personnel must log a minimum of three (3) active operational hours per month directly recorded within the state database to maintain vehicle spawn permissions, good standing, and active employment status.

Q6: How do I verify my clearance for heavy equipment and specialized trailers?

Operators can check their authorized fleet access dynamically by visiting the internal Vehicle Guidelines page and filtering by their verified certifications (CDL, RB, WK, HVY, Trailer, or Flag).

Q7: What are the strict appearance mandates regarding uniform components and textures?

Operators must strictly match the component and texture index numbers listed on the internal Uniform Guidelines page for their specific rank tier (Cadet, Field Ops, Supervisor, or Command). In addition, possessing pets or animals on shift is strictly prohibited.

Q8: How is command structure established when arriving at a multi-agency scene?

On any scene featuring active emergency responders, law enforcement holds absolute Scene Commander authority. SADOT units must remain near their vehicles and await explicit instructions until the scene is officially cleared and command of traffic management is formally transferred to our senior on-site unit.
